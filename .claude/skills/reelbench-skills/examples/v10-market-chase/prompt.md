> **What changed from v9, after checking the actual v9 clip frame-by-frame:**
> 1. **Cut the vault-over-the-fruit-stall shot (old Shot 3) entirely.** Frame evidence from v9
>    showed old Shot 3 and old Shot 4 (the handkerchief lift) rendering in swapped order — the
>    lift appeared on screen (~2.5–4.0s) *before* the vault (~4.5–5.2s), reversed from the script.
>    Worse, the vault itself didn't render as a clean athletic leap across five straight
>    generations (v5–v9) — it kept rendering as a spin-kick *into* the stall, reading as an attack
>    rather than an escape move. The user's own observation across all five generations ("she
>    always jumps at the fruit stall") confirms this is a real, repeatable model bias: an
>    ambiguous non-combat action dropped into a fight-heavy sequence keeps getting reinterpreted
>    as combat. Removing the beat removes both failure modes at once rather than continuing to
>    patch wording that hasn't held across five tries.
> 2. **Moved the incidental stall damage into Shot 1** as a brief, in-motion, non-action detail
>    (her passing shoulder clips a stall edge while running, spilling a little fruit) so the later
>    shots (fall, escape) still have scattered fruit/broken pottery to land in/reference, without
>    a dedicated shot competing for the model's attention or reading as deliberate combat.
> 3. **Renumbered every shot** (9 shots now, was 10) and recalculated every timestamp. All prior
>    fixes are retained: negative constraints folded into the end of `detailed_description` (not
>    a separate field), `retention_analysis` as a comma-separated shot list, global style sentence
>    before `[Shot 1]`, causal-order lock on the lift→reaction pair (now Shot 3→Shot 4), the kick
>    (not a shove), and the "โอ้ย!" line on the face-shock reaction.
> 4. Total scripted duration is now 12.1s for 9 shots — this pipeline's actual output length has
>    NOT been a reliable fixed value across this project's own tests (v4–v7 all returned 16.5s;
>    v9 returned 14.375s instead), so 12.1s is simply a sane total for 9 shots, not a tuned target.
